# Vertical Video Feed Prototype - DESIGN.md

## Purpose
Prototype a white-label vertical video feed for editorial media brands.
The prototype should work across multiple brands without redesigning the core product structure.

## Product principles
- Video-first
- Fast, immersive, swipeable
- Trusted publisher feel
- Minimal UI chrome
- Monetisable without ruining immersion
- White-label and token-friendly

## Experience rules
- One full-screen vertical video per viewport
- Swipe vertically to move through feed
- Keep overlays lightweight
- Metadata and source must remain understandable
- UI may fade when inactive
- Actions remain reachable with one hand
- Safe-area aware for mobile devices
- Captions must remain readable at all times
- Ad overlays must not block critical content or captions

## Core MVP states
- Organic editorial video
- Sponsored in-feed video
- Organic video with lower-third overlay ad

## Future / out of scope for now
- Comments / reactions sheet
- Publisher / topic / series detail pages
- Full article transition pages
- Save / bookmark library
- Advanced personalization UI

## Visual direction
- Neutral dark UI
- Inter or similar sans-serif
- Generic outlined icon set
- Minimal colour accents
- No brand-specific assets
- Components should be easily themeable later

## White-label constraints
- No hardcoded brand colours
- No hardcoded brand logos
- Typography, colour, and spacing should later map to tokens
- Structure should remain stable across BILD, WELT, Buzz, and future brands

## Content assumptions
- Videos may belong to editorial publishers, topics, or campaigns
- Ads must be clearly distinguishable from editorial content
- Feed content may later be personalized, but MVP UI should work without advanced recommendation logic

## Prototype goal
Create a believable, fast, mobile-first concept for stakeholder review and early implementation discussion.
The prototype should focus on the core feed experience and not on full platform functionality.
